# Product identity

## Product name

Acta

## Tagline

Give operations teams deterministic control over repetitive browser workflows.

## Voice

Write in clear, precise technical English. Be confident, concrete, and free of exaggerated marketing claims.

## Shared principles

- Prioritize precision over novelty.
- Preserve user autonomy, review, and control.
- State limitations honestly.
- Keep the product focused on its own problem territory.

## Naming rule for future products

Prefer distinctive, memorable names inspired by Latin or Romance languages that express the product's purpose. Avoid generic, fashionable, or category-only names.
